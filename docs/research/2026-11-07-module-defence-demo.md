## 1. Responsibility

A defence demonstration is a **5-minute, air-gap-safe, falsifiable arc**: the candidate shows a claim, the jury re-runs its acceptance command, and one break attempt proves the gate that defends it. The arc is bounded by what is VERIFIED on the builder host and explicitly excludes every BLOCKED live row (§5.1). Two repo realities define its shape:

1. **What it is.** The frozen corpus (159 runs / 528 rows / 208 valid / 74 degraded / 246 invalid / 22 cells, verified by `book/tools/audit-archive.py`) is never re-measured, re-graded, or rewritten — the rebuild replaces the *implementation* of the deliverable modules and harmonizes the book with what the code actually is (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-cgo-enterprise-rebuild-design.md:10-21`). The methodology is the factoriel plan profiles × qdiscs × CC × repetitions with per-event protocol baseline/charge/recovery and validity gates G0–G7 (`docs/methodology.fr.md:120-142`, `docs/methodology.fr.md:146-196`). The soutenance format is a 15–20 min presentation + 20–30 min questions in the French model, jury 2–4 members (`docs/defense-research.md:12-15`); the Q&A carries a significant share of the grade, with 15–20 prepared questions and 5–10 backup slides (`docs/defense-research.md:142-157`).
2. **What it is not.** The demo never measures a live link, never loads traffic, never shapes, never re-seals. Enterprise tariffs are contractual scenarios, not measurements (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-cgo-enterprise-rebuild-design.md:30-31`, `docs/VERIFY.md:48` `RECORDED`); the 220 ms deadline is the DGM operator objective, never a WMO mandate (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-cgo-enterprise-rebuild-design.md:58-60`); `bbr` in the thesis means BBRv1 (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-cgo-enterprise-rebuild-design.md:61-62`); audit reports say “achieved saturation throughput”, not “link capacity” (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-cgo-enterprise-rebuild-design.md:66-67`).

What each primary source **legitimately** requires of a standalone demo tool — and what it does not require.

### 4.1 Dublin Descriptors, second cycle (R1)

The EUT+ page reproduces the Joint Quality Initiative (2004) *Shared ‘Dublin’ descriptors* and the EQF (2008): second cycle (Master’s) requires **90–120 ECTS, with at least 60 ECTS at the second-cycle level**, with expected outcomes: **advanced knowledge that allows for original thinking; ability to solve problems in new and unfamiliar environments; integration of multidisciplinary knowledge; capacity for critical analysis and ethical reflection; high level of autonomy in research and learning** (R1, “Second Cycle (Master’s Level)”). The descriptors were developed as common learning-outcome criteria to ensure compatibility and comparability among national qualifications in the EHEA, defined in terms of workload, level/outcomes, competences, and graduate profile (R1, “Purpose” + “Background and Bologna Process Alignment”). The EQF comprises eight levels defined by knowledge, skills (cognitive and practical), and responsibility/autonomy (R1, “The European Qualifications Framework”). National self-certification requires designating competent national bodies, demonstrating clear links between national qualifications and Bologna cycle descriptors, having QA systems consistent with the Berlin Communiqué, and including alignment references in diploma supplements (R1, “The self-certification process”, citing Maguire 2011); Ireland's pilot compared its NFQ against the Bologna descriptors for Bachelor’s, Master’s, and Doctorate levels (R1, “Ireland’s Experience with Bologna Alignment”).

**Consequence for `cgo-demo`:** the demo **is** the “new and unfamiliar environment” exhibit (R1): the jury runs the acceptance commands on their own host, not the candidate's. “Original thinking” (R1) must be claimed in the *measurement method* (reproducible bench + gates + seal), not in queueing theory — fq_codel/CAKE are specified standards (`docs/methodology.fr.md:11-12` cites RFC 8290 / RFC 7928; the thesis trace-driven chapter names pfifo_fast, fq_codel, CAKE and cubic, bbr, bbr3 as the replayed disciplines and CCs, with BBRv3 *not measured*, `docs/thesis_trace_driven_methodology.md:10-13`, `docs/thesis_trace_driven_methodology.md:123-130`). Multidisciplinary integration (R1) is already the project's shape (kernel bench + AQM standards + enterprise economics + service APIs); the demo must show each strand meeting its own primary spec, not blur them.

### 4.2 WMO WIS 2.0 (R2)

From the WMO Community Knowledge Hub WIS2 Overview page (R2): WIS 2.0 officially entered its **operational phase 1 January 2025**, marking the transition from GTS to WIS2 (R2, “WIS 2.0 Implementation Plan”). Unlike GTS, which relies on private dedicated links, **WIS 2.0 leverages the public Internet and employs a publish-subscribe model using the MQTT protocol** (R2, head). Each data publisher must operate a **WIS2 Node** publishing data/metadata notifications and enabling downloads via HTTP(S); a Node is composed of an **MQTT broker** (publishes WIS2 notifications) plus an **HTTP server** (enables download of data and metadata) (R2, “What is a WIS2 Node?”). The three Global Services are **Global Broker** (MQTT(S) re-publishing notifications of all Nodes), **Global Discovery Catalogue** (API/web discovery of datasets), **Global Cache** (HTTP(S) core-data download), and **Global Monitor** (dashboards + alerting for 24/7 operations) (R2, “WIS2 Global Services”). Registration must be approved by the **Permanent Representative (PR)** for the country/territory and submitted by the **National Focal Point (NFP)**; an operational Node must have a publicly available MQTT endpoint regularly publishing WIS2 Notifications to pass the initial assessment by the **principal GISC** (R2, “WIS2 Node Registration”, Notes 1–2). For accelerated implementation WMO supports **“WIS2 in a box” (wis2box)**, a FOSS reference implementation of a WIS2 Node (R2, “Setting up a WIS 2.0 Node”).

**Consequence for `cgo-demo`:** CGO is a **link auditor/bench**, WIS2 is the **transport**, CAP-style payloads are the **alert format** — the product boundary must be stated in the demo, never blurred. The DGM scenario the demo serves is bulk download versus alert egress: the enterprise spec names *“200 MB EUMETSAT downlink vs CAP egress”* as the actual DGM download case and measures Model C as *loaded 16 KB completions at 220 ms; breach flag `< 95%`* (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-cgo-enterprise-rebuild-design.md:134-136`, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-cgo-enterprise-rebuild-design.md:151-153`). The 220 ms figure is the **DGM operator objective**, never a WMO mandate: the repo invariant states WMO-No. 1061 defines WIS 2.0 “real time” as “a few seconds to a few minutes” with no numeric per-alert threshold (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-cgo-enterprise-rebuild-design.md:58-60`); the WMO-No. 1061 full text was not fetched in this pass — the numeric “few seconds to minutes” quote is therefore repo-cited, primary-verified status `unverified — primary source not found` at the direct-fetch level. The demo must never present CGO as a WIS2 Node or CAP producer: only an operational MQTT-publishing Node can be registered (R2, Note 2), and CGO publishes no MQTT notifications.

## 5. Gap analysis (current → releasable `cgo-demo`)

Ordered by severity. Each gap cites local evidence; primary-source side cites R# or is `unverified — primary source not found`.

1. **No dedicated demo sequencer exists — the 5-min arc lives in prose, not in a runnable artefact.** The arc (§1) is described in master-demonstration research §5 and implicitly in `docs/defense-research.md:53-60`, but no command runs it end-to-end (gates → oracle → seal → mutate → figures → verdict) and prints a one-verdict sheet. Today a candidate must remember six gate names, two oracle lines, the seal check, the temp-copy mutation, and the fixture verdict by hand. Per R1 (solve problems in new/unfamiliar environments), the jury's environment is unfamiliar by definition — a sequencer that *is* the unfamiliar-environment exhibit is required, not a slide list. Fix: `cgo-demo demo` runs the arc in order, prints expected-vs-observed per step, and ends with a falsification offer; `cgo-demo doctor-verify` runs a single named gate with the same verdict mapping.
2. **SKIP honesty — the demo must never turn “not run” into green.** `gate_frozen` SKIPs (exit-2 path) when `book/` or the audit script is missing (`tools/verify.py:65-66`); the VERIFY row notes the resulting detail “says ‘book/ absent’ inaccurately” when `book/` exists but the script or corpus is missing (sibling evidence-seal research §5.2; `docs/VERIFY.md:26`). The six-gate verdict returns 2 if any gate SKIPs (`tools/verify.py:35-36`). A demo that prints “not run” in a green font has already failed the ledger rule that `RECORDED` is not promoted and 2 is not green (`docs/VERIFY.md:83-91`). Fix: the demo maps absent→exit 2 naming the absence, partial/corrupt→exit 1 naming the file, and refuses exit 0 on a partial tree.
3. **`ProvenanceSHA`/`hash8` covers one file, not the corpus.** `ProvenanceSHA` hashes only the lexically-last `aqm_eval.csv` (`pkg/figures/figures.go:41-53`); mutating any of the other 158 runs does not move it, renaming a run changes which file is “last” (sibling evidence-seal research §5.3). `provenanceMeta` embeds `time.Now().UTC()` and a local path, so two `figures` runs are not byte-identical (`pkg/figures/figures.go:63-79`). The methodology calls this “triple provenance” across figures, dashboard, and reports (`docs/methodology.fr.md:231-238`) while the seal research shows it is the weakest link. Per R1 critical analysis, the demo must present `hash8` as what it is (last-file label) and bind the corpus digest (sorted `path + sha256`, seal shape) as the real corpus subject. Fix: `cgo-demo` computes both, names them honestly, and emits the corpus digest into the verdict sheet.
4. **Verdict/honesty invariants are unit-fixture-only in the demo's reach.** “No load ⇒ no grade” and “unmeasured ⇒ `Unavailable`, never zero, never a pass” are invariants (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-cgo-enterprise-rebuild-design.md:40-44`) and the cascade row is VERIFIED on unit fixtures only — “Never observed against a real audit” (`docs/VERIFY.md:63`). ## 6. Target tool — `cgo-demo` (independent demo repo; `doctor-verify` surface)

Single-purpose: it *sequences the falsifiable arc and states its scope*, it never measures, shapes, re-seals, or claims live results. Repo `cgo-demo`, one Go binary `cgo-demo` with the `doctor-verify` identity as its first surface (shell alias `cgo-doctor-verify` → `cgo-demo doctor-verify` for the name in the brief). Per R1 it must run in the jury's environment; per R2 it must state the WIS2/CAP boundary; per R3 it must mark ISGEI items as questions, not facts.

```
cgo-demo doctor-verify [--only frozen|provenance|tests|exit|hygiene|links] [--scope frozen-only|live]
                       → 0 iff the named gate passes with 2 OK lines (frozen) / clean fault proof (selftest path);
                         1 on mismatch/corrupt (names the run/file); 2 on absent corpus or named BLOCKED live scope (never green)
cgo-demo demo          → runs the §1 five steps in order, prints expected-vs-observed per step and the falsification command,
                         ends with the scope sheet (frozen VERIFIED rows, BLOCKED rows with conditions, fixture-only note)
cgo-demo figures-check → regenerates SVGs from data/runs, prints last-file SHA-256 + hash8 + corpus digest (sorted path+sha256),
                         diffs the two (the §5.3 honesty exhibit)
cgo-demo version       → prints tag version + corpus digest; exit 0
```

Design invariants (each kills one §5 gap):

1. **Pure core, CLI adapter.** Port `seal_findings`/`gate_frozen`/`prove_frozen` (`tools/verify.py:48-128`) and `verifyManifests` (`cmd/cgo/main.go:375-431`) nearly verbatim into Go internals; clock, subprocess, and network stay in `cmd/` adapters only (gap §5.1).
2. **Verdict mapping is the product.** Absent → exit 2 naming the absence; partial/corrupt → exit 1 naming the file; never a zero count read as a measurement (gap §5.2). A green run on a partial tree is a false claim against `docs/VERIFY.md:83-91`.
3. **Two provenance labels, never one.** `last-file SHA-256` + `hash8` (label, 32 bits) *and* corpus digest (sorted manifest hash, seal shape) — the verdict sheet names both and states which is the corpus subject (gap §5.3; R1 critical analysis).
4. **Fixture-only is printed, not implied.** The verdict step's output carries the exact honesty qualifier from `docs/VERIFY.md:63` (“unit fixtures only, never observed against a real audit”) (gap §5.4).
5. **Live scope is a refusal, not a surprise.** `--scope live` prints the BLOCKED condition (Linux 6.8, ≥7.1 kernel, ≥30-sample trace, QEMU CHR, operator grant a–d) and exits non-zero without it (gap §5.5; human-approval gate per AGENTS.md “Stop and ask”).
6. **Fixed boundary line.** Every `demo` and `doctor-verify` output ends with the §5.7 boundary sentence (CGO = auditor/bench; WIS2 = transport; no Node, no producer, no WMO mandate) and the §5.6/§4.3 `règlement` annex pointer (gaps §5.6, §5.7; R1, R2, R3).
7. **Audit is shelled, not re-implemented.** Its stdout/exit are quoted into the verdict sheet so the verdict is replayable (gap §5.1; captures “this is reproducible” in one screen).

Module shape: independent Go module with its own `go.mod`, `go 1.25` to match `go.mod:3`, stdlib only for the demo core (`crypto/sha256`, `encoding/hex`, `encoding/json`, `os`, `path/filepath`). No `embed` of the corpus, no vendor strings, no shaping/measurement code. Tag `vX.Y.Z`; never move a published tag (R1 alignment-reference requirement: the verdict sheet cites the tag).

## 8. Anti-toy criteria (what would make this a toy — and the bar for “not a toy”)

A `cgo-demo` submission is a toy if it *slides* a demo instead of falsifying one. It is **not** a toy only if all of the following hold:

1. **Independent reproduction on the jury's host.** `cgo-demo doctor-verify` reproduces `159 runs / 528 lines / 208 valid / 74 degraded / 246 invalid / 22 cells` (`docs/VERIFY.md:24`), the seal 159-OK (`docs/VERIFY.md:26`), and the integrity counts `208/74/246` (`docs/VERIFY.md:51`) — not just its own fixtures. If a counting-rule change moves any of those numbers without a named `VERIFIED` row, it rewrites history.
2. **Fault-proof parity, jury-triggered.** Every witness in `prove_frozen` (`tools/verify.py:82-128`) and `--selftest` (`tools/verify.py:510-516`) is ported and still catches: rewritten manifest (audit passes, seal catches), mutated data file (audit catches), added/deleted run, absent anchor — with the intact-copy control passing; and the jury is *invited* to inject one mutation during the arc (`docs/defense-research.md:140-157`, Q&A weighting). A verifier without a fault proof is an opinion.
3. **Provenance honesty, Dublin-typed (R1).** The tool declares both provenance labels (last-file `hash8`, corpus digest), labels the verdict step `fixture-only` (`docs/VERIFY.md:63`), names all BLOCKED live rows with conditions (`docs/VERIFY.md:28-30`, `:52-53`, `:57`, `:60`), and never presents any of them, the enterprise scenarios (`docs/VERIFY.md:48` `RECORDED`), or the last-file `hash8` (`pkg/figures/figures.go:41-53`) as a live/corpus measurement. CAKE/MikroTik claims and the WMO-No. 1061 numeric quote stay `unverified — primary source not found` at fetch level until their primaries are fetched.
4. **No silent skip, no silent zero, no green SKIP.** Partial tree, corrupt file, skipped run, absent `book/`, and Windows `n/a` doctor lines (`pkg/doctor/doctor.go:50-51`, `:124-126`) are five distinct named answers, never a zero count read as a measurement. If two corpora can yield the same verdict with no way to tell which files were skipped, it is a helper, not a demo.
5. **Verify-from-a-clean-clone + scope discipline.** A fresh clone (no `book/`) answers exit 2 naming the absence — never exit 0 — and the §5.7 boundary line (CGO = auditor/bench; WIS2 = transport; no Node, no producer, no WMO mandate) travels with every output. The tool must survive the case-study posture: nothing executes beyond the frozen corpus without the named human/operator approval (AGENTS.md “Stop and ask”; `docs/VERIFY.md:60` grant condition).
6. **Version discipline + ISGEI honesty.** Declared API (§2), immutable `vX.Y.Z` tags, verdict sheet citing the tag, and an annex listing every R3-missing item (`unverified — primary source not found`) as a question to the secretariat — never a fabricated rubric or word count. A repo that slides French-blog grid practice (`docs/defense-research.md:421-425`) as an ISGEI requirement is a toy regardless of code quality.
